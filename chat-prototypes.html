3 · Timeline

Treats the session as a work log rather than a dialogue. Every prompt, tool call, reply and approval is a node on a vertical rail, colored by status and timestamped. You can tell where time went and where things failed without reading any prose.

  • Best for supervising long autonomous runs
  • Status visible in peripheral vision
  • Prose feels secondary; noisier for Q&A chats

4 · Conversation + Activity

The conversation holds only human-readable messages. Tool calls, outputs and diffs stream into a dedicated activity pane on the right, in the style of an IDE debug console. Inline refs in a message jump to the matching activity row. Approvals pin to the bottom bar.

  • Clean reading and deep inspection at the same time
  • Diffs and logs get proper width and mono space
  • Needs a wide window; two places to look

5 · Turn cards + action dock

Each prompt and its outcome form one card. The card shows your ask as a title, a compact progress strip with one segment per tool call (red where one failed), the agent's answer, and chips for the files it touched. Past turns collapse to their title. Pending approvals rise into a dock above the composer, which keeps y/n in thumb's reach.

  • Maps to how you think: "what did I ask, what changed"
  • Collapsed history makes long sessions skimmable
  • Most custom rendering work
